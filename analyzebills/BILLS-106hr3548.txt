                                 A BILL


 
To redesignate the mud dump site located approximately 6 miles east of 
 Sandy Hook, New Jersey, and known as the ``Historic Area Remediation 
          Site'', as the ``Albert Gore, Jr., Mud Dump Site''.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. REDESIGNATION.

    The dredged materials disposal area located approximately 6 miles 
east of Sandy Hook, New Jersey, and known as the ``Historic Area 
Remediation Site'' shall be known and designated as the ``Albert Gore, 
Jr., Mud Dump Site''.

SEC. 2. REFERENCES.

    Any reference in law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other 
record of the United States to the site referred to in section 1 shall 
be deemed to be a reference to the ``Albert Gore, Jr., Mud Dump Site''.
