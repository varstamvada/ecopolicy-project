                                 A BILL


 
     To provide construction assistance for a project for a water 
  transmission line from the Missouri River to the city of Williston, 
                             North Dakota.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. WILLISTON, NORTH DAKOTA.

    Section 219(f) of the Water Resources Development Act of 1992 (106 
Stat. 4835; 113 Stat. 334) is amended by adding at the end the 
following:
            ``(44) Williston, north dakota.--$3,900,000 for a project 
        for a water transmission line from the Missouri River to the 
        city of Williston, North Dakota.''.
