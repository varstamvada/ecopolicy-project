                                 A BILL


 
 To reauthorize the National Landslide Preparedness Act, and for other 
                               purposes.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``National Landslide Preparedness Act 
Reauthorization Act of 2024''.

SEC. 2. REAUTHORIZATION OF THE NATIONAL LANDSLIDE PREPAREDNESS ACT.

    (a) National Landslide Hazards Reduction Program.--Section 3(h) of 
the National Landslide Preparedness Act (43 U.S.C. 3102(h)) is 
amended--
            (1) in the matter preceding paragraph (1), by striking 
        ``2024'' and inserting ``2034''; and
            (2) in paragraph (1), by striking ``$25,000,000 to carry 
        out this section'' and inserting ``$40,000,000 to carry out 
        this section, of which not less than $15,000,000 shall be made 
        available for the purchase and deployment of landslide early 
        warning systems in high-risk areas''.
    (b) 3d Elevation Program.--Section 5(e) of the National Landslide 
Preparedness Act (43 U.S.C. 3104(e)) is amended by striking ``2024'' 
and inserting ``2034''.
