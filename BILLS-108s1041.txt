                                 A BILL


 
To prohibit oil and gas drilling in Finger Lakes National Forest in the 
                           State of New York.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. PROHIBITION ON OIL AND GAS DRILLING IN FINGER LAKES NATIONAL 
              FOREST IN NEW YORK.

    No Federal permit or lease shall be issued for oil or gas drilling 
in Finger Lakes National Forest in the State of New York.
