                                 A BILL


 
 To prohibit the Secretary of Agriculture from making payments to the 
                      Brazilian Cotton Institute.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. PROHIBITION ON PAYMENTS TO BRAZILIAN COTTON INSTITUTE.

    The Secretary of Agriculture may not directly, or through the 
Commodity Credit Corporation, make payments to the Brazilian Cotton 
Institute.
