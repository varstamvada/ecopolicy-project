                                 A BILL


 
To reauthorize funding for the Urban Search and Rescue Response System.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``National Urban Search and Rescue 
Response System Reauthorization Act''.

SEC. 2. REAUTHORIZATION OF FUNDING.

    Section 634(b) of the Post-Katrina Emergency Management Reform Act 
of 2006 (6 U.S.C. 722(b)) is amended by striking ``for fiscal year 
2008'' and inserting ``for each of the fiscal years 2018 through 
2022''.
