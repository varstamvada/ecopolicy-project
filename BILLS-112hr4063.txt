                                 A BILL


 
To repeal section 512 of the Credit Card Accountability Responsibility 
and Disclosure Act of 2009 which relates to carrying certain weapons in 
                            National Parks.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Guns-Free National Parks Act of 
2012''.

SEC. 2. REPEAL OF PROVISION THAT RELATES TO CARRYING CERTAIN WEAPONS IN 
              NATIONAL PARKS.

    Section 512 of the Credit Card Accountability Responsibility and 
Disclosure Act of 2009 (16 U.S.C. 1a-7b) is repealed.
