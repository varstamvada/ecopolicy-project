                                 A BILL


 
 To amend the Fort Peck Reservation Rural Water System Act of 2000, to 
        extend the authorization of appropriations for that Act.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. FORT PECK RESERVATION RURAL WATER SYSTEM, MONTANA.

    Section 9 of the Fort Peck Reservation Rural Water System Act of 
2000 (Public Law 106-382; 114 Stat 1457), is amended by striking, 
``over a period of 10 fiscal years'' each place it appears and 
inserting ``through fiscal year 2015''.
