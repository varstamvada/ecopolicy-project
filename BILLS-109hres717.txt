                               RESOLUTION


 
    Directing the Secretary of Commerce to transmit to the House of 
Representatives a copy of a workforce globalization final draft report 
               produced by the Technology Administration.

    Resolved, That the Secretary of Commerce is directed to transmit to 
the House of Representatives, not later than 14 days after the date of 
the adoption of this resolution, a copy of the final draft report, 
produced by the professional staff of the Technology Administration, 
entitled: ``Six-Month Assessment of Workforce Globalization In Certain 
Knowledge-Based Industries''.
