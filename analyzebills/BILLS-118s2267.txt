 following bill; which was read twice and referred to the Committee on

_______________________________________________________________________

                                 A BILL


 
  To amend the Agriculture Improvement Act of 2018 to reauthorize the 
                 dairy business innovation initiatives.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Dairy Business Innovation Act of 
2023''.

SEC. 2. DAIRY BUSINESS INNOVATION INITIATIVES.

    Section 12513(i) of the Agriculture Improvement Act of 2018 (7 
U.S.C. 1632d(i)) is amended by striking ``$20,000,000'' and inserting 
``$36,000,000''.
