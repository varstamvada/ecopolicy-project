                                 A BILL


 
  To terminate funding for the Fast Flux Test Facility at the Hanford 
                   Nuclear Reservation in Washington.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. FAST FLUX TEST FACILITY AT HANFORD NUCLEAR RESERVATION.

    The Secretary of Energy shall make no expenditure, for any fiscal 
year beginning with fiscal year 2003, for the continued operation of 
the Fast Flux Test Facility at the Hanford Nuclear Reservation in 
Washington. Nothing in this section shall prohibit funds from being 
used for decommissioning, clean-up, removal of waste, treating of 
waste, or dismantling of such Test Facility.
