                                 A BILL


 
To increase the Federal share for transportation projects for the State 
                              of Michigan.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. INCREASED FEDERAL SHARE FOR TRANSPORTATION PROJECTS.

    In reposonse to the serious economic downturn in the State of 
Michigan, the Secretary of Transportation shall increase the Federal 
share to 100 percent for any project or program carried out under title 
23, United States Code, or chapter 53 of title 49, United States Code, 
for such State for fiscal years 2010 and 2011.
