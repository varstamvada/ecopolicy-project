                                 A BILL


 
 To authorize a project for environmental restoration at Smith Island, 
                               Maryland.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SMITH ISLAND, MARYLAND.

    The project for environmental restoration, Smith Island, Maryland, 
is authorized to be carried out by the Secretary of the Army 
substantially in accordance with the plans, and subject to the 
conditions, described in the Report of the Chief of Engineers dated 
October 29, 2001, at a total cost of $7,442,000, with an estimated 
Federal cost of $4,838,000 and an estimated non-Federal cost of 
$2,604,000.
