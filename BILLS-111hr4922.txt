                                 A BILL


 
To amend the Energy Policy Act of 2005 to repeal a section of that Act 
         relating to exportation or importation of natural gas.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. EXPORTATION OR IMPORTATION OF NATURAL GAS.

    (a) In General.--Section 311 of the Energy Policy Act of 2005 
(Public Law 109-58; 119 Stat. 685) is repealed.
    (b) Application.--The Natural Gas Act (15 U.S.C. 717 et seq.) shall 
be applied and administered as if section 311 of the Energy Policy Act 
of 2005 (and the amendments made by the section) had not been enacted.
