                                 A BILL


 
 To amend the Richard B. Russell National School Lunch Act to increase 
 the fine for certain fraudulent activities related to funds for child 
              nutrition programs, and for other purposes.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Fighting Their Fraud Act of 2023''.

SEC. 2. PREVENTION OF FRAUD UNDER CHILD NUTRITION PROGRAMS.

    Section 12(g) of the Richard B. Russell National School Lunch Act 
(42 U.S.C. 1760(g)) is amended by striking ``$25,000'' and inserting 
``$50,000''.
