                                 A BILL


 
To specify the amount of Federal funds that may be expended for intake 
 facilities for the benefit of Lonoke and White Counties, Arkansas, as 
  part of the project for flood control, Greers Ferry Lake, Arkansas.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. GREERS FERRY LAKE, ARKANSAS.

    Section 117 of the Miscellaneous Appropriations Act, 2001 (114 
Stat. 2763A-228), is amended by inserting before the period at the end 
the following: ``, at a total Federal cost of not more than 
$6,000,000''.
