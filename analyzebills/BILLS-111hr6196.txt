                                 A BILL


 
   To amend title 23, United States Code, to modify the deadline for 
    filing a claim seeking judicial review of a permit, license, or 
      approval issued by a Federal agency for a highway or public 
        transportation capital project, and for other purposes.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. DEADLINE FOR CLAIMS RELATING TO HIGHWAY AND PUBLIC 
              TRANSPORTATION CAPITAL PROJECTS.

    Section 139(l) of title 23, United States Code, is amended by 
striking ``180 days'' in each of paragraphs (1) and (2) and inserting 
``90 days''.
