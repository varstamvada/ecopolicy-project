                                 A BILL


 
   To give Executive Order 13547, relating to the stewardship of the 
 ocean, the coasts, and the Great Lakes, the full force and effect of 
                      law, and for other purposes.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``National Ocean Policy Act of 2018''.

SEC. 2. LEGAL EFFECT OF EXECUTIVE ORDER 13547.

    (a) Legal Effect.--Executive Order 13547 (75 Fed. Reg. 43021), 
entitled ``Stewardship of the Ocean, Our Coasts, and the Great Lakes'', 
signed on July 19, 2010, shall have the full force and effect of law.
    (b) Conforming Change.--The Executive Order entitled ``Executive 
Order Regarding the Ocean Policy to Advance the Economic, Security, and 
Environmental Interests of the United States'', signed on June 19, 
2018, shall have no force or effect.
