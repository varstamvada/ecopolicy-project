                                 A BILL


 
   To amend title 49, United States Code, with respect to electronic 
                logging devices, and for other purposes.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``ELD Extension Act of 2017''.

SEC. 2. ELECTRONIC LOGGING DEVICES.

    (a) In General.--Section 31137(b)(1)(C) of title 49, United States 
Code, is amended by striking ``2 years'' and inserting ``4 years''.
    (b) Revision of Regulations.--The Secretary of Transportation shall 
modify regulations issued under section 31137 of title 49, United 
States Code, as necessary to carry out the amendment made by this 
section.
