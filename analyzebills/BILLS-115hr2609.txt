                                 A BILL


 
   To direct the Secretary of the Army to revise certain authorized 
  purposes described in the Missouri River Mainstem Reservoir System 
                      Master Water Control Manual.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. MASTER WATER CONTROL MANUAL REVISION.

    Not later than 90 days after the date of enactment of this Act, the 
Secretary of the Army, acting through the Chief of Engineers, shall 
revise the Missouri River Mainstem Reservoir System Master Water 
Control Manual, and any related regulations, to delete fish and 
wildlife as an authorized purpose of the Corps.
