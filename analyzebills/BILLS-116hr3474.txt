                                 A BILL


 
To amend title 49, United States Code, to include truck combinations in 
the definition of automobile transporter for purposes of certain length 
                              limitations.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. LENGTH LIMITATIONS FOR AUTOMOBILE TRANSPORTERS.

    Section 31111(a)(1) of title 49, United States Code, is amended by 
inserting ``and truck tractors attached to trailers designed for the 
transportation of assembled highway vehicles'' after ``camper units''.
