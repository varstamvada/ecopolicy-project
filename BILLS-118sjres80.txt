                            JOINT RESOLUTION


 
  Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, 
United States Code, of the rule submitted by the United States Fish and 
 Wildlife Service relating to ``Endangered and Threatened Wildlife and 
 Plants; Regulations Pertaining to Endangered and Threatened Wildlife 
                             and Plants''.

    Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United 
States of America in Congress assembled, That Congress disapproves the 
rule submitted by the United States Fish and Wildlife Service relating 
to ``Endangered and Threatened Wildlife and Plants; Regulations 
Pertaining to Endangered and Threatened Wildlife and Plants'' (89 Fed. 
Reg. 23919 (April 5, 2024)), and such rule shall have no force or 
effect.
