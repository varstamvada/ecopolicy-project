                                 A BILL


 
 To amend title I of the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 
  1968 to include private firefighters and rescue squad and ambulance 
                   crew members for certain benefits.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. BENEFITS FOR CERTAIN PRIVATE SAFETY OFFICERS.

    Paragraph (7) of section 1204 of title I of the Omnibus Crime 
Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 (42 U.S.C. 3796b(7)) is amended--
            (1) by striking the period and inserting ``and ;'' and
            (2) by adding at the end the following:
                    ``(D) any firefighter, rescue squad or ambulance 
                crew member who is performing official duties for a 
                private company or organization that has a contractual 
                agreement with a public agency to provide fire 
                suppression, rescue, or ambulance services for such 
                agency.''.
