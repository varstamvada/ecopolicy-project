                                 A BILL


 
 To amend the National Landslide Preparedness Act to reauthorize such 
                                  Act.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``National Landslide Preparedness Act 
Reauthorization Act of 2024''.

SEC. 2. NATIONAL LANDSLIDE PREPAREDNESS ACT REAUTHORIZATION.

    The National Landslide Preparedness Act (43 U.S.C. 3101 et seq.) is 
amended--
            (1) in section 3(h) (43 U.S.C. 3102(h)), by striking ``2021 
        through 2024'' and inserting ``2024 through 2028''; and
            (2) in section 5(e), by striking ``2021 through 2024'' and 
        inserting ``2024 through 2028''.
