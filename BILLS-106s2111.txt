                                 A BILL


 
To direct the Secretary of Agriculture to convey for fair market value 
 1.06 acres of land in the San Bernardino National Forest, California, 
              to KATY 101.3 FM, a California corporation.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION. 1. SALE OF LAND TO KATY 101.3 FM, A CALIFORNIA CORPORATION.

    (a) In General.--Subject to valid existing rights and settlement of 
claims as provided in this section, the Secretary of Agriculture 
(referred to in this section as the ``Secretary'') shall convey to KATY 
101.3 FM, a California corporation (referred to in this section as 
``KATY''), for fair market value, all right, title, and interest of the 
United States in and to the land described in subsection (b).
    (b) Land Description.--
            (1) In general.--The land referred to in subsection (a) is 
        the approximately 1.06 acres of land in the San Bernardino 
        National Forest, Riverside County, California, generally 
        located in the north half of section 23, Township 5 South, 
        Range 2 East, San Bernardino Meridian, as generally depicted as 
        Exhibit A-2 in an appraisal report of the land dated August 26, 
        1999.
            (2) Legal description.--The Secretary and KATY shall agree 
        on the legal description of the land.
    (c) Consideration.--
            (1) In general.--Consideration for the conveyance under 
        subsection (a) shall be equal to the appraised fair market 
        value of the property.
            (2) Appraisal standards.--An appraisal to determine fair 
        market value under paragraph (1) shall be--
                    (A) prepared in conformity with the Uniform 
                Appraisal Standards for Federal Land Acquisitions; and
                    (B) approved by the Secretary.
    (d) Costs.--Any costs associated with the creation of a subdivided 
parcel, recordation of a survey, and zoning and planning approval and 
similar expenses shall be paid by KATY.
    (e) Access.--Notwithstanding section 1323(a) of the Alaska National 
Interest Lands Conservation Act (16 U.S.C. 3210(a)) or any other law, 
the Secretary shall not be required to provide access over the National 
Forest System land to the land conveyed under subsection (a).
    (f) Release.--On payment by KATY to the Secretary of $16,600, the 
Secretary shall release KATY from all claims of the United States 
arising from the occupancy and use of San Bernardino National Forest 
land by KATY for communication site purposes.
    (g) Use of Funds.--All funds received by the Secretary under this 
section--
            (1) shall be deposited in the fund established under Public 
        Law 90-171 (commonly known as the ``Sisk Act'') (16 U.S.C. 
        484a); and
            (2) shall remain available until expended for the 
        acquisition of land, water, and interests in land or water for 
        the San Bernardino National Forest, California.
