                                 A BILL


 
   To amend the Bend Pine Nursery Land Conveyance Act to specify the 
 recipients and consideration for conveyance of the Bend Pine Nursery, 
                        and for other purposes.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. MODIFICATION OF BEND PINE NURSERY LAND CONVEYANCE.

    Section 3 of the Bend Pine Nursery Land Conveyance Act (Public Law 
106-526; 114 Stat. 2512) is amended--
            (1) in subsection (a)(1), by striking ``210 acres'' and 
        inserting ``185 acres'';
            (2) in subsection (e), by striking paragraph (3); and
            (3) by adding at the end the following new subsection:
    ``(g) Special Requirements for Bend Pine Nursery Conveyance.--
            ``(1) Conveyance to park and recreation district.--The 
        Secretary shall offer to sell to the Bend Metro Park and 
        Recreation District in Deschutes County, Oregon, for 
        consideration in the amount of $3,505,676, all right, title, 
        and interest of the United States in and to approximately 170 
        acres of the parcel of land identified as Tract A in subsection 
        (a)(1). The land conveyed to the Bend Metro Park and Recreation 
        District shall be used only for recreation purposes and may be 
        developed for those purposes.
            ``(2) Conveyance to school district.--In accordance with 
        section 202 of the Education Land Grant Act (16 U.S.C. 479a), 
        the Secretary shall convey to the Administrative School 
        District No. 1, Deschutes County, Oregon, without 
        consideration, all right, title, and interest of the United 
        States in and to approximately 15 acres of land in the 
        northwest corner of the parcel of land identified as Tract A in 
        subsection (a)(1). The land conveyed to the School District 
        shall be used only for educational purposes.''.
