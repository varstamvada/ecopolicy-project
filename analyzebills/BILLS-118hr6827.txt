                                 A BILL


 
 To amend the Richard B. Russell National School Lunch Act to increase 
      commodity assistance under the summer food service program.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. COMMODITY ASSISTANCE UNDER THE SUMMER FOOD SERVICE PROGRAM.

    Section 13(h) of the Richard B. Russell National School Lunch Act 
(42 U.S.C. 1761(h)) is amended by adding at the end the following: 
``The value of donated foods distributed to service institutions 
pursuant to this subsection shall be equal to the national average 
value of donated foods determined under section 6(c)(1)(A).''
