                                 A BILL


 
  To limit the amount of funds available to the Aeronautics Research 
       Mission Directorate of the National Aeronautics and Space 
  Administration during fiscal year 2007 and fiscal years thereafter.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. LIMITATION ON FUNDS AVAILABLE TO AERONAUTICS RESEARCH 
              MISSION DIRECTORATE OF NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE 
              ADMINISTRATION DURING FISCAL YEAR 2007 AND FISCAL YEARS 
              THEREAFTER.

    Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the total amount of 
funds available to the Aeronautics Research Mission Directorate of the 
National Aeronautics and Space Administration during any fiscal year 
after fiscal year 2006 may not exceed $724,000,000.
