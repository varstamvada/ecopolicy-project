                                 A BILL


 
   To reauthorize the National Oilheat Research Alliance Act of 2000.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``National Oilheat Research Alliance 
Reauthorization Act of 2009''.

SEC. 2. REAUTHORIZATION.

    Section 713 of the National Oilheat Research Alliance Act of 2000 
(42 U.S.C. 6201 note; Public Law 106-469) is amended by striking ``the 
date that is 9 years after the date on which the Alliance is 
established'' and inserting ``February 6, 2011''.
