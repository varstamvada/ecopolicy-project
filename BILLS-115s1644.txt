                                 A BILL


 
  To clarify the status of the Captain John Smith Chesapeake National 
         Historic Trail as a unit of the National Park System.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Captain John Smith Chesapeake 
National Historic Trail Parity Act''.

SEC. 2. CAPTAIN JOHN SMITH CHESAPEAKE NATIONAL HISTORIC TRAIL.

    Section 5(a)(25)(C) of the National Trails System Act (16 U.S.C. 
1244(a)(25)(C)) is amended, in the matter preceding clause (i), by 
inserting ``as a unit of the National Park System'' after ``the 
Secretary of the Interior''.
