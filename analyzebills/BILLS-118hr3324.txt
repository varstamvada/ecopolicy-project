Public Law 118-166
118th Congress

                                 An Act


 
 To extend the authority to collect Shasta-Trinity Marina &lt;&lt;NOTE: Dec. 
        23, 2024 -  [H.R. 3324]&gt;&gt; fees through fiscal year 2029.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. SHASTA-TRINITY MARINA FEES.

    Section 422 of the Department of the Interior, Environment, and 
Related Agencies Appropriations Act, 2008 (Public Law 110-161; 121 Stat. 
2149) as amended, is further amended by striking ``and each subsequent 
fiscal year through fiscal year 2019'' and inserting ``and each 
subsequent fiscal year through fiscal year 2029''.

    Approved December 23, 2024.
