                                 A BILL


 
Making appropriations for certain Federal housing programs in the event 
                       of a Government shutdown.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Affordable Housing Stability During 
Shutdowns Act of 2023''.

SEC. 2. FUNDING FOR SECTION 8 RENTAL ASSISTANCE VOUCHERS DURING 
              GOVERNMENT SHUTDOWN.

    There are hereby appropriated for fiscal year 2024, out of any 
money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, such sums as are 
necessary to provide assistance, during the period of a lapse in 
discretionary appropriations beginning on or after the date of the 
enactment of this section and at a rate for operations and under the 
terms and conditions provided for such assistance in fiscal year 2023, 
under the following programs:
            (1) The housing choice voucher program for rental 
        assistance under section 8(o) of the United States Housing Act 
        of 1937 (42 U.S.C. 1437f(o)), including the program under 
        paragraph (13) of such section for project-based voucher 
        assistance.
            (2) The program for project-based rental assistance under 
        section 8 of such Act (42 U.S.C. 1437f).
            (3) The program for assistance for supportive housing for 
        the elderly under section 202 of the Housing Act of 1959 (12 
        U.S.C. 1701q).
            (4) The program for assistance for supportive housing for 
        persons with disabilities under section 811 of the Cranston-
        Gonzalez National Affordable Housing Act (42 U.S.C. 8013).
            (5) The program for rental assistance for rural multifamily 
        housing under title V of the Housing Act of 1949 (42 U.S.C. 
        1471 et seq.).
