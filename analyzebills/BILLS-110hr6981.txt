                                 A BILL


 
      To make technical corrections to section 4406 of the Food, 
                 Conservation, and Energy Act of 2008.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. ASSISTANCE FOR COMMUNITY FOOD PROJECTS.

    (a) Section 4406(a)(7) of the Food, Conservation, and Energy Act of 
2008 (Public Law 110-234; 112 Stat. 1902) is amended by striking ``Food 
and Nutrition Act of 2008'' and inserting ``Food Stamp Act of 1977''.
    (b) Funds allocated under section 25(b) of the Food Stamp Act of 
1977 (7 U.S.C. 2034(b)) for fiscal year 2008 shall remain available 
until September 30, 2009, to fund proposals solicited in fiscal year 
2008.
