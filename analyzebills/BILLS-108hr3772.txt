                                 A BILL


 
    To include Nelson County and Franklin County, Virginia, in the 
  Appalachian region for purposes of the programs of the Appalachian 
                          Regional Commission.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. INCLUSION OF NELSON COUNTY AND FRANKLIN COUNTY, VIRGINIA, IN 
              THE APPALACHIAN REGION.

    Section 14102(a)(1)(L) of title 40, United States Code, is 
amended--
            (1) by inserting ``Franklin,'' after ``Floyd,''; and
            (2) by inserting ``Nelson,'' after ``Montgomery,''.
