                                 A BILL


 
To designate the visitor center for the First State National Historical 
 Park to be located at the Sheriff's House in New Castle, Delaware, as 
                the ``Thomas R. Carper Visitor Center''.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. DESIGNATION OF THE VISITOR CENTER FOR THE FIRST STATE 
              NATIONAL HISTORICAL PARK IN NEW CASTLE, DELAWARE, AS THE 
              ``THOMAS R. CARPER VISITOR CENTER''.

    (a) Designation.--The visitor center for the First State National 
Historical Park to be located at the Sheriff's House in New Castle, 
Delaware, or any successor to that visitor center that is constructed 
after the date of enactment of this Act and serves as the primary 
visitor center for the First State National Historical Park, shall be 
known and designated as the ``Thomas R. Carper Visitor Center''.
    (b) References.--Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, 
paper, or other record of the United States to the visitor center 
referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the 
``Thomas R. Carper Visitor Center''.
