                                 A BILL


 
 To amend the Reclamation Projects Authorization and Adjustment Act of 
1992 to modify the City of West Jordan, Utah, Reuse Project to include 
        recycling and reuse of naturally impaired surface water.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION. 1. MODIFICATION OF CITY OF WEST JORDAN, UTAH, REUSE PROJECTS.

    (a) In General.--Section 1625(a) of the Reclamation Projects 
Authorization and Adjustment Act of 1992 (43 U.S.C. 390h-12k) is 
amended by inserting before the period the following: ``and naturally 
impaired surface water''.
