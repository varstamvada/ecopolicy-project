                                 A BILL


 
  To rename the Homestead National Monument of America near Beatrice, 
          Nebraska, as the Homestead National Historical Park.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. HOMESTEAD NATIONAL HISTORICAL PARK.

    (a) Renaming.--The unit of the National Park System known as the 
Homestead National Monument of America and located near Beatrice, 
Nebraska, shall be known as the ``Homestead National Historical Park''.
    (b) References.--Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, 
paper, or other record of the United States to the unit of the National 
Park System know as the Homestead National Monument of America shall be 
deemed to be a reference to the ``Homestead National Historical Park''.
