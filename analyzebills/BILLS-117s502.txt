                                 A BILL


 
  To amend chapter 53 of title 49, United States Code, to incorporate 
   zero-emission fueling technology into the definition of ``capital 
                               project''.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``More Access to ZEV Equipment in 
Transit Act'' or the ``MAZE in Transit Act''.

SEC. 2. ZERO-EMISSION FUELING TECHNOLOGY.

    Section 5302(3)(G)(v) of title 49, United States Code, is amended--
            (1) by redesignating subclauses (XII), (XIII), and (XIV) as 
        subclauses (XIII), (XIV), and (XV), respectively; and
            (2) by inserting after subclause (XI) the following:
                                    ``(XII) technology to fuel a zero 
                                emission vehicle (as defined in section 
                                5339(c));''.
