                                 A BILL


 
To include phosphate and potash on the final list of critical minerals 
                   of the Department of the Interior.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. INCLUSION OF PHOSPHATE AND POTASH AS CRITICAL MINERALS.

    The list of critical minerals published in the notice of the 
Secretary of the Interior entitled ``2022 Final List of Critical 
Minerals'' (87 Fed. Reg. 10381 (February 24, 2022)) shall be deemed to 
include phosphate and potash.
