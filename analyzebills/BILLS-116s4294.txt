                                 A BILL


 
  To provide supplemental appropriations to the Community Development 
          Financial Institutions Fund, and for other purposes.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Coronavirus CDFI Funding Act''.

SEC. 2. SUPPLEMENTAL APPROPRIATION.

    There is appropriated to the Community Development Financial 
Institutions Fund established under section 104(a)(1) of the Community 
Development Banking and Financial Institutions Act of 1994 (12 U.S.C. 
4703(a)(1)), out of amounts in the general fund of the Treasury not 
otherwise appropriated, $1,000,000,000 for fiscal year 2020, for 
providing financial assistance and technical assistance under 
subparagraphs (A) and (B) of section 108(a)(1) of the Community 
Development Banking and Financial Institutions Act of 1994 (12 U.S.C. 
4707(a)(1)), except that subsections (d) and (e) of such section 108 
shall not apply to the provision of such assistance.
