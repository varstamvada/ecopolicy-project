                                 A BILL


 
 To amend the Low-Income Home Energy Assistance Act of 1981 to address 
   the needs of low-income households in States with high needs for 
      cooling assistance, by adjusting a hold harmless provision.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. ALLOTMENT OF FUNDS TO STATES THAT NEED COOLING ASSISTANCE.

    Section 2604(a)(2)(A)(ii) of the Low-Income Home Energy Assistance 
Act of 1981 (42 U.S.C. 8623(a)(2)(A)(ii)) is amended by striking 
``$1,975,000,000'' and inserting ``$1,000,000,000''.
