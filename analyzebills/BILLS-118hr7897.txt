                                 A BILL


 
To require the Secretary of the Interior to finalize a proposed rule to 
    remove the Apache trout from the Federal List of Endangered and 
                          Threatened Wildlife.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. FINALIZATION OF PROPOSED RULE TO REMOVE APACHE TROUT FROM 
              FEDERAL LIST OF ENDANGERED AND THREATENED WILDLIFE.

    Not later than 90 days after the date of the enactment of this 
section, the Secretary of the Interior, acting through the Director of 
the United States Fish and Wildlife Service, shall finalize the 
proposed rule titled ``Endangered and Threatened Wildlife and Plants; 
Removing the Apache Trout From the List of Endangered and Threatened 
Wildlife'' (88 Fed. Reg. 54548; published August 11, 2023).
