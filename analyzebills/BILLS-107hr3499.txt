                                 A BILL


 
 To expand the Farm Storage Facility Loan Program of the Department of 
Agriculture by making loans available to assist producers in providing 
                            storage for hay.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. FARM STORAGE FACILITY LOAN PROGRAM.

    The Secretary of Agriculture shall expand the Farm Storage Facility 
Loan Program of the Department of Agriculture to include hay as one of 
the agricultural commodities for which loans are available to assist 
producers in providing storage for the commodity.
