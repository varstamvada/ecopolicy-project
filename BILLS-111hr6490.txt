                                 A BILL


 
   To amend the Soda Ash Royalty Reduction Act of 2006 to extend the 
                   reduced royalty rate for soda ash.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. EXTENSION OF REDUCTION IN ROYALTY RATE ON SODA ASH.

    Section 102 of the Soda Ash Royalty Reduction Act of 2006 (Public 
Law 109-338; 30 U.S.C. 262 note) is amended by striking ``5-year'' and 
inserting ``10-year''.
