                                 A BILL


 
To amend title 49, United States Code, to repeal the security screening 
                 opt-out program for airport operators.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Passenger Security Act of 2004''.

SEC. 2. SECURITY SCREENING OPT-OUT PROGRAM.

    Section 44920 of title 49, United States Code, and the item 
relating to such section in the analysis for chapter 449 of such title, 
are repealed.
