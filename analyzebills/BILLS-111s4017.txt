                                 A BILL


 
                     To amend the CDBG service cap.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. CDBG SERVICE CAP ADJUSTMENT.

    Section 105(a)(8) of the Housing and Community Development Act of 
1974 (42 U.S.C. 5305(a)(8)) is amended by inserting before the 
semicolon the following: ``, and except that in each of fiscal years 
2011 and 2012, the limitation set forth in this paragraph shall be 
applied by substituting `25 percent' for `15 percent';''.
