                                 A BILL


 
  To eliminate the use of the Savannah River nuclear waste separation 
                     facilities in South Carolina.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SAVANNAH RIVER NUCLEAR WASTE SEPARATION FACILITIES.

    The Secretary of Energy shall terminate the use of the Savannah 
River nuclear waste separation facilities in South Carolina as a 
nuclear waste separation facility for spent nuclear fuel and other 
irradiated material--
            (1) at the beginning of fiscal year 2003, or
            (2) on the date the spent nuclear fuel and other irradiated 
        material at such facilities has been separated,
whichever occurs first.
