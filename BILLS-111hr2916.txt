                                 A BILL


 
To provide that no recreation grants made using funds from the Land and 
      Water Conservation Fund may be used to acquire land or make 
                 improvements in State or local parks.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. LIMITATION ON FUNDING OF NATIONAL HERITAGE AREAS.

    Notwithstanding any other law, no recreation grants made using 
funds from the Land and Water Conservation Fund may be used to acquire 
land or make improvements in State or local parks.
