                                 A BILL


 
   To extend the moratorium enacted by the Internet Tax Freedom Act.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Internet Growth and Freedom Act of 
2003''.

SEC. 2. AMENDMENT.

    Section 1101(a) of the Internet Tax Freedom Act (47 U.S.C. 151 
note) is amended by striking ``2003'' and inserting ``2008''.
