                                 A BILL


 
To amend the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 to modify the thrifty food 
   plan to apply to the urban and rural parts of the State of Hawaii.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Feeding Rural Families Act of 
2023''.

SEC. 2. AMENDMENT.

    Section 3(u)(2) of the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 (7 U.S.C. 
2012(u)(2)) is amended--
            (1) by inserting ``urban and rural parts of'' before 
        ``Hawaii'' the 1st place it appears, and
            (2) by inserting ``urban and rural'' before ``Hawaii'' the 
        2d place it appears.
