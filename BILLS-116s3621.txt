                                 A BILL


 
   To amend the Safe Drinking Water Act to remove limitations on the 
 required use of American iron and steel products in projects carried 
     out using State revolving loan funds, and for other purposes.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Made in America Water Infrastructure 
Act of 2020''.

SEC. 2. AMERICAN IRON AND STEEL PRODUCTS IN PROJECTS USING DRINKING 
              WATER STATE REVOLVING LOAN FUNDS.

    Section 1452(a)(4)(A) of the Safe Drinking Water Act (42 U.S.C. 
300j-12(a)(4)(A)) is amended by striking ``During fiscal years 2019 
through 2023, funds'' and inserting ``Funds''.
