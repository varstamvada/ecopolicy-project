                                 A BILL


 
   To amend the Department of the Interior, Environment, and Related 
 Agencies Appropriations Act, 2008, to terminate the authority of the 
    Secretary of the Treasury to deduct amounts from certain States.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHARE OF OIL AND GAS LEASING REVENUES.

    The matter under the heading ``administrative provisions'' under 
the heading ``Minerals Management Service'' of title I of the 
Department of the Interior, Environment, and Related Agencies 
Appropriations Act, 2008 (Subdivision F of Public Law 110-161) is 
amended by striking the second undesignated paragraph.
