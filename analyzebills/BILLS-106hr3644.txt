                                 A BILL


 
  To authorize drawdown and distribution from the Strategic Petroleum 
Reserve in the case of severe emergency supply interruptions on a State 
                           or regional level.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. AMENDMENT.

    Section 161(d)(2)(C) of the Energy Policy and Conservation Act (42 
U.S.C. 6241(d)(2)(C)) is amended by inserting ``, or on a State or 
regional economy'' after ``the national economy''.
