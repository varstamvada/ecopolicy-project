                                 A BILL


 
 To redesignate the Cottonwood Visitor Center at Joshua Tree National 
        Park as the ``Senator Dianne Feinstein Visitor Center''.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. REDESIGNATION OF THE COTTONWOOD VISITOR CENTER AT JOSHUA 
              TREE NATIONAL PARK AS THE ``SENATOR DIANNE FEINSTEIN 
              VISITOR CENTER''.

    (a) Redesignation.--The Cottonwood Visitor Center at Joshua Tree 
National Park shall be known and designated as the ``Senator Dianne 
Feinstein Visitor Center''.
    (b) References.--Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, 
paper, or other record of the United States to the visitor center 
referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the 
``Senator Dianne Feinstein Visitor Center''.
