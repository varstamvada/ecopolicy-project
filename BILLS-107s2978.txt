                                 A BILL


 
To modify the project for flood control, Little Calumet River, Indiana.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. MODIFICATION OF FLOOD CONTROL PROJECT, LITTLE CALUMET RIVER, 
              INDIANA.

    The project for flood control, Little Calumet River, Indiana, 
authorized by section 401(a) of the Water Resources Development Act of 
1986 (100 Stat. 4115), is modified to authorize the Secretary of the 
Army to construct the project substantially in accordance with the 
post-authorization change report of the Corps of Engineers dated August 
2000, at a total cost of $187,000,000, with an estimated Federal cost 
of $137,000,000 and an estimated non-Federal cost of $50,000,000.
