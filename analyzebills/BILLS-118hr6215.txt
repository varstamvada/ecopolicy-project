                                 A BILL


 
 To prohibit Federal funds from being made available for the Justice40 
             Initiative or for the American Climate Corps.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``No American Climate Corps Act''.

SEC. 2. AMERICAN CLIMATE CORPS AND JUSTICE40 INITIATIVE FUNDING 
              PROHIBITION.

    No Federal funds may be used for--
            (1) the Justice40 Initiative (as described in Executive 
        Order 14008 (86 Fed. Reg. 7619, relating to tackling the 
        climate crisis)); or
            (2) the American Climate Corps (as described in the fact 
        sheet published by the White House on September 20, 2023, 
        relating to the American Climate Corps).
