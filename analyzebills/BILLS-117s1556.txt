                                 A BILL


 
 To require a report on the feasibility and benefits of establishing a 
                   supply chain center of excellence.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SUPPLY CHAIN CENTER OF EXCELLENCE.

    Not later than 1 year after the date of the enactment of this Act, 
the Administrator of the National Aeronautics and Space Administration 
(referred to in this section as ``NASA'') shall submit to the Committee 
on Commerce, Science, and Transportation of the Senate and the 
Committee on Science, Space, and Technology of the House of 
Representatives a report on the feasibility and benefits of 
establishing a supply chain center of excellence in a State in which 
NASA does not, as of the date of the enactment of this Act, have a 
research center or test facility.
