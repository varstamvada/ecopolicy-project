                            JOINT RESOLUTION


 
To disapprove the rule submitted by the Environmental Protection Agency 
   on July 13, 2000, relating to total maximum daily loads under the 
                  Federal Water Pollution Control Act.

    Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United 
States of America in Congress assembled, That Congress disapproves the 
rule submitted by the Environmental Protection Agency on July 13, 2000 
(volume 65, number 135, page 43586 of the Federal Register), relating 
to total maximum daily loads under the Federal Water Pollution Control 
Act, and such rule shall have no force or effect.
