                                 A BILL


 
 To amend the Low-Income Home Energy Assistance Act of 1981 to provide 
      for an additional allocation of funds to the insular areas.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. ADDITIONAL ALLOCATION OF FUNDS TO INSULAR AREAS.

    Section 2604 of the Low-Income Home Energy Assistance Act of 1981 
(42 U.S.C. 8623) is amended--
            (1) in subsection (a)(1)(A), by striking ``subsection 
        (b)(1)'' and inserting ``subsection (b)(1)(A)''; and
            (2) in subsection (b)(1)--
                    (A) by striking ``(b)(1)'' before ``The Secretary 
                shall apportion not less than'' and inserting 
                ``(b)(1)(A)'';
                    (B) by striking ``under this paragraph'' and 
                inserting ``under this subparagraph'', each place it 
                appears; and
                    (C) by adding at the end the following new 
                subparagraph:
    ``(B) Beginning in fiscal year 2013, and for each fiscal year 
thereafter, the Secretary shall allocate not less than 30 percent of 
the amount appropriated pursuant to section 2602(d) for such fiscal 
year to the jurisdictions to which subparagraph (A) applies to provide 
supplementary funds to such jurisdictions for carrying out this title 
on the basis of need among such jurisdictions.''.
