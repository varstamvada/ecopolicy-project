                                 A BILL


 
 To prevent Federal funds from being used to carry out Executive Order 
                     13925, and for other purposes.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Defund Executive Orders that 
Suppress Free Speech Act''.

SEC. 2. PROHIBITION ON USE OF FEDERAL FUNDS TO IMPLEMENT EXECUTIVE 
              ORDER 13925.

    No Federal funds may be obligated or expended for the salaries and 
expenses of any Federal employee who implements Executive Order 13925 
(85 Fed. Reg. 34079; relating to Preventing Online Censorship).
