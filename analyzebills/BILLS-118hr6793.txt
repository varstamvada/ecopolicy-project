                                 A BILL


 
   To prohibit further action on the proposed rule entitled ``Energy 
Conservation Program: Energy Conservation Standards for Ceiling Fans''.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Small Business Ceiling Fan 
Manufacturer Protection Act''.

SEC. 2. PROHIBITING IMPLEMENTATION OF PROPOSED RULE ENTITLED ``ENERGY 
              CONSERVATION PROGRAM: ENERGY CONSERVATION FOR CEILING 
              FANS''.

     The Secretary of Energy may not take any action to finalize, 
implement, or enforce the proposed rule entitled ``Energy Conservation 
Program: Energy Conservation Standards for Ceiling Fans'' (88 Fed. Reg. 
40932 (June 22, 2023)) or any future proposed rule of a substantially 
similar nature.
