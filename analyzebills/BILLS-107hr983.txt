                                 A BILL


 
   To require the Secretary of Energy to assign the same priority to 
 providing renewable energy production incentive payments for landfill 
gas facilities as the priority assigned to providing such payments for 
                       other biomass facilities.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. LANDFILL GAS FACILITIES.

    Section 1212(a) of the Energy Policy Act of 1992 (42 U.S.C. 
13317(a)) is amended by adding at the end the following: ``The 
Secretary shall assign the same priority to payments for landfill gas 
facilities as to payments for closed loop biomass facilities.''.
