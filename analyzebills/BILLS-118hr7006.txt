                                 A BILL


 
 To prohibit natural asset companies from entering into any agreement 
 with respect to land in the State of Utah or natural assets on or in 
                               such land.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. PROHIBITION.

    (a) In General.--A natural asset company may not enter into any 
agreement with respect to land in the State of Utah or natural assets 
on or in such land.
    (b) Natural Asset Company Defined.--In this section, the term 
``natural asset company''--
            (1) has the meaning given that term under the notice of the 
        Securities and Exchange Commission titled ``Self-Regulatory 
        Organizations; New York Stock Exchange LLC; Notice of Filing of 
        Proposed Rule Change To Amend the NYSE Listed Company Manual To 
        Adopt Listing Standards for Natural Asset Companies'' (88 Fed. 
        Reg. 68811; published October 4, 2023); and
            (2) includes any company that is substantially similar to a 
        company described under paragraph (1).
