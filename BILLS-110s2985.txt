                                 A BILL


 
  To amend the Safe, Accountable, Flexible, Efficient Transportation 
  Equity Act: A Legacy for Users to correct a reference relating to a 
             transit project in Orleans Parish, Louisiana.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. PROJECT MODIFICATION, ORLEANS PARISH, LOUISIANA.

    The description for item 67 in section 3044(a) of the Safe, 
Accountable, Flexible, Efficient Transportation Equity Act: A Legacy 
for Users (Public Law 109-59) is amended to read as follows: ``Union 
Passenger Terminal Planning and Master Plan and Infrastructure 
Improvements in Orleans Parish, Louisiana''.
