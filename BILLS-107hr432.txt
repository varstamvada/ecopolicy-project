                                 A BILL


 
To authorize State and local governments to regulate, for public safety 
               purposes, trains that block road traffic.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Railroad Crossing Delay Safety 
Assurance Act''.

SEC. 2. AUTHORITY.

    If the Secretary of Transportation has not, before August 1, 2002, 
issued regulations to address the problem of trains blocking road 
traffic, a State or local government may enact a law or regulation to 
address such problem for the purpose of ensuring public safety.
