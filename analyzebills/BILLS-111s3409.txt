                                 A BILL


 
 To make certain adjustments to the price analysis of propane prepared 
                     by the Secretary of Commerce.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. AMENDMENT.

    Section 9(a) of the Propane Education and Research Act of 1996 (15 
U.S.C. 6408(a)) is amended by striking ``price of consumer grade 
propane to a composite'' and inserting ``average price of consumer 
grade propane to all end users to an equally weighted composite''.

SEC. 2. UPDATED PRICE ANALYSIS.

    Not later than 60 days after the date of enactment of this Act, the 
Secretary of Commerce shall prepare and make available to the public a 
propane price analysis reflecting the amendment made by section 1.
