                                 A BILL


 
To reauthorize funding for the Solid Waste Infrastructure for Recycling 
         Grant Program of the Environmental Protection Agency.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Solid Waste Infrastructure for 
Recycling Grant Program Reauthorization Act''.

SEC. 2. REAUTHORIZATION OF SOLID WASTE INFRASTRUCTURE FOR RECYCLING 
              GRANT PROGRAM.

    Section 302(g)(1) of the Save Our Seas 2.0 Act (33 U.S.C. 
4282(g)(1)) is amended by inserting ``and $65,000,000 for each of 
fiscal years 2026 through 2035'' after ``fiscal years 2021 through 
2025''.
