                            JOINT RESOLUTION


 
  Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, 
    United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Department of 
    Transportation relating to ``Metropolitan Planning Organization 
                Coordination and Planning Area Reform''.

    Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United 
States of America in Congress assembled, That Congress disapproves the 
rule submitted by the Department of Transportation relating to 
``Metropolitan Planning Organization Coordination and Planning Area 
Reform'' (81 Fed. Reg. 93448 (December 20, 2016)), and such rule shall 
have no force or effect.
