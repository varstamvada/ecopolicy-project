                                 A BILL


 
To amend title 23, United States Code, to include education on trailer 
                safety in State highway safety programs.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Trailer Safety Improvement Act''.

SEC. 2. TRAILER SAFETY PROGRAMS.

    Section 402(a)(2)(A)(xiv) of title 23, United States Code, is 
amended by inserting ``, prevent improper and unsafe use of light-duty 
and medium-duty trailers, and educate the public about required trailer 
safety equipment and preventive maintenance'' after ``unsecured vehicle 
loads''.
