                                 A BILL


 
     To appropriate $500 million to the low-income household water 
 assistance program under section 2912 of the American Rescue Plan Act 
                    of 2021, and for other purposes.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Water Access Act''.

SEC. 2. APPROPRIATION FOR LIHWAP.

    There is appropriated to the Secretary of Health and Human Services 
for fiscal year 2024, $500,000,000 for the low-income household water 
assistance program under section 2912 of subtitle K of the American 
Rescue Plan Act of 2021 (15 U.S.C. 9058b).
