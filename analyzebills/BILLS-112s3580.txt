                                 A BILL


 
To require the Corps of Engineers to preserve the historical integrity 
of Zoar, Ohio, while carrying out any study relating to or construction 
  of flood damage reduction measures, including levees, in Zoar, Ohio.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. CONDITIONS RELATING TO FLOOD DAMAGE REDUCTION MEASURES IN 
              ZOAR, OHIO.

    The Secretary of the Army, acting through the Chief of Engineers, 
shall preserve in place, consistent with the Standards for Preservation 
and Guidelines for Preserving Historic Buildings, published by the 
Secretary of the Interior, the historical integrity of Zoar, Ohio, 
while carrying out any study relating to or construction of flood 
damage reduction measures, including levees, in Zoar, Ohio.
