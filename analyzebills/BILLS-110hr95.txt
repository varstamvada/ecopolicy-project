                                 A BILL


 
  To make funds available for fiscal years 2007 and 2008 for program 
   integrity purposes, including the data mining project, under the 
                      Federal Crop Insurance Act.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. CROP INSURANCE PROGRAM INTEGRITY.

    For each of fiscal years 2007 and 2008, the Secretary of 
Agriculture may use not more than $3,600,000 of funds made available 
under section 522(e) of the Federal Crop Insurance Act (7 U.S.C. 
1522(e)) for program integrity purposes, including the data mining 
project.
