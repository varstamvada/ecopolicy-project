                                 A BILL


 
  To amend the Agricultural Act of 2014 to make available additional 
   funds for the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands pilot 
                                project.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Northern Mariana Islands Enhanced 
Nutrition Assistance Act of 2017''.

SEC. 2. AMENDMENT.

    Section 4031(e)(2)(A)(ii) of the Agricultural Act of 2014 (Public 
Law 113-79; 48 U.S.C. 1841 note) is amended by striking ``and 2018'' 
and inserting ``through 2023''.
