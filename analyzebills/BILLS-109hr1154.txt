                                 A BILL


 
  To provide for the retention of the name of the geologic formation 
known as ``Devils Tower'' at the Devils Tower National Monument in the 
                           State of Wyoming.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled, That, notwithstanding 
any other authority of law, the mountain located 44 deg.42'58" N., by 
104 deg.35'32" W., shall continue to be named and referred to for all 
purposes as Devils Tower.
