                                 A BILL


 
  To direct the heads of Federal agencies and departments to publish 
 certain data with respect to disaster relief, and for other purposes.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Natural Disaster Transparency 
Enhancement Act''.

SEC. 2. PUBLICATION OF DISASTER RELIEF DATA.

    The head of each Federal agency or department that approves Federal 
funds for natural disaster relief shall publish and regularly update on 
the website of such agency or department a breakdown of--
            (1) the amount of funds approved with respect to each 
        disaster; and
            (2) the amount of funds disbursed with respect to each 
        disaster.
