                                 A BILL


 
To amend the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act to direct the 
  Secretary of Agriculture to create the USDA Express Guaranteed Farm 
Ownership and Guaranteed Farm Operating Loan programs, enabling faster 
          access to credit for America's farmers and ranchers.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``USDA Express Loan Act''.

SEC. 2. USDA EXPRESS LOANS.

    (a) In General.--Subtitle A of the Consolidated Farm and Rural 
Development Act (7 U.S.C. 1922 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end 
the following:

``SEC. 310J. USDA EXPRESS GUARANTEED FARM OWNERSHIP LOAN PROGRAM.

    ``(a) In General.--Within 1 year after the date of the enactment of 
this section, the Secretary shall establish a program, which shall be 
known as the `USDA Express Guaranteed Farm Ownership Loan Program', 
under which, within 36 hours after receipt of an application to 
guarantee a farm ownership loan that meets the requirement of 
subsection (e) originated by a USDA express lender, the Secretary shall 
notify the lender as to whether the application is approved or 
disapproved.
    ``(b) USDA Express Lender Defined.--In this section, the term `USDA 
express lender' means any regulated lender authorized by the Secretary 
to participate in the guaranteed farm ownership loan program under this 
subtitle.
    ``(c) Documents.--In originating a loan for consideration under 
this section, a USDA express lender may use any loan documents that 
incorporate the loan analyses, procedures, and documentation of the 
lender, or such other documents as the Secretary may prescribe.
    ``(d) Credit Decisions.--Before submitting an application pursuant 
to this section, a USDA express lender shall determine the 
creditworthiness, and ascertain the repayment ability, of the borrower 
involved.
    ``(e) Loans Eligible for Guarantee.--The Secretary shall not accept 
an application under this section with respect to a loan that exceeds 
$1,000,000 per farm or ranch operation per year.
    ``(f) Maximum Guarantee.--The percentage of the principal amount of 
a loan which may be guaranteed pursuant to this section shall not 
exceed--
            ``(1) 75 percent, in the case of a loan not exceeding 
        $750,000; or
            ``(2) 50 percent, in the case of a loan of more than 
        $750,000 and not more than $1,000,000.
    ``(g) Use of Loan Proceeds.--The borrower of a loan guaranteed 
pursuant to this section shall use the loan proceeds only to produce 
agricultural commodities consistent with the purposes of the guaranteed 
loan programs under this title.
    ``(h) Regulations.--The Secretary shall issue such regulations as 
are necessary to implement this section.''.
    (b) Section 312 of such Act (7 U.S.C. 1942) is amended by adding at 
the end the following:
    ``(f) USDA Express Guaranteed Farm Operating Loans.--The Secretary 
shall establish a USDA Express Guaranteed Farm Operating Loan Program 
on the same basis as the USDA Express Guaranteed Farm Ownership Loans 
Program under section 310J.''.
