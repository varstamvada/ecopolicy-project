                                 A BILL


 
To pay personnel compensation and benefits for employees of the Federal 
                        Aviation Administration.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Federal Aviation Employees 
Protection Act of 2011''.

SEC. 2. EXPENDITURE AUTHORITY FOR PERSONNEL COSTS.

     (a) In General.--Section 9502(d)(1) of the Internal Revenue Code 
of 1986 is amended--
            (1) by striking ``before July 23, 2011,'';
            (2) in subparagraph (A), by inserting ``before July 23, 
        2011,'' after ``incurred'';
            (3) in subparagraph (B)--
                    (A) by striking ``heretofore or hereafter'' and 
                inserting ``before July 23, 2011,'' after ``incurred''; 
                and
                    (B) by striking ``or'' at the end of clause (iv);
            (4) in subparagraph (C), by striking ``.'' and inserting 
        ``; or''; and
            (5) by adding at the end the following:
                    ``(D) incurred under the Federal Aviation Employees 
                Protection Act of 2011.''.
    (b) Effective Date.--The amendments made by this section shall take 
effect on July 23, 2011.

SEC. 3. USE OF TRUST FUND.

    Notwithstanding the expiration of section 48103(8) of title 49, 
United States Code, amounts in the Airport and Airway Trust Fund shall 
be available to pay personnel compensation and benefits for employees 
of the Federal Aviation Administration funded from appropriations or 
authorizations provided on or before July 22, 2011.
