                                 A BILL


 
  To designate the bridge located in Blount County, Tennessee, on the 
Foothills Parkway (commonly known as ``Bridge 2'') as the ``Dean Stone 
                               Bridge''.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. DESIGNATION.

    The bridge located in Blount County, Tennessee, on the Foothills 
Parkway (commonly known as ``Bridge 2'') shall be known and designated 
as the ``Dean Stone Bridge''.

SEC. 2. REFERENCES.

    Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other 
record of the United States to the bridge referred to in section 1 
shall be deemed to be a reference to the ``Dean Stone Bridge''.
