                                 A BILL


 
 To amend the Federal Water Pollution Control Act to extend the pilot 
             program for alternative water source projects.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. PILOT PROGRAM FOR ALTERNATIVE WATER SOURCE PROJECTS.

    Section 220(j) of the Federal Water Pollution Control Act (33 
U.S.C. 1300(j)) is amended in the first sentence--
            (1) by striking ``$75,000,000'' and inserting 
        ``$125,000,000''; and
            (2) by striking ``2002 through 2004'' and inserting ``2006 
        through 2010''.
