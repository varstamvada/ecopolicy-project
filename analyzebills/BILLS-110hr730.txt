  To provide funds to certain State and local governments to pay for 
 utility costs resulting from the provision of temporary housing units 
  to evacuees from Hurricane Katrina and other hurricanes of the 2005 
                                season.

United States of America in Congress assembled,

    Section 2401 of the Emergency Supplemental Appropriations Act for 
Defense, the Global War on Terror, and Hurricane Recovery, 2006 (Public 
Law 109-234), shall be applied by substituting ``2008'' for the date 
specified in such section.
