                                 A BILL


 
To permit the waiver of Jones Act requirements for humanitarian relief 
                                efforts.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Humanitarian Disaster Relief Act of 
2017''.

SEC. 2. JONES ACT WAIVER FOR HUMANITARIAN RELIEF EFFORTS.

    Section 501(b) of title 46, United States Code, is amended by 
inserting ``or humanitarian relief efforts'' after ``national defense'' 
in each place it appears.
