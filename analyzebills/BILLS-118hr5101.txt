                                 A BILL


 
To increase the reference prices for certain commodities, and for other 
                               purposes.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Agricultural Commodities Price 
Enhancement Act of 2023''.

SEC. 2. REFERENCE PRICE INCREASES FOR WHEAT, CORN, SOYBEANS, PEANUTS, 
              AND SEED COTTON.

    Section 1111(19) is amended of the Agricultural Act of 2014 (7 
U.S.C. 9011(19)) is amended--
            (1) in subparagraph (A), by striking ``$5.50 per bushel'' 
        and inserting ``$6.05 per bushel'';
            (2) in subparagraph (B), by striking ``$3.70 per bushel'' 
        and inserting ``$4.63 per bushel'';
            (3) in subparagraph (H), by striking ``$8.40 per bushel'' 
        and inserting ``$9.66 per bushel'';
            (4) in subparagraph (J), by striking ``$535.00 per ton'' 
        and inserting ``$588.50 per ton''; and
            (5) in subparagraph (O), by striking ``$0.367 per pound'' 
        and inserting ``$0.45 per pound''.
