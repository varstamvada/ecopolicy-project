                                 A BILL


 
To amend the exemption of certain legal requirements applied to Amtrak 
 in making improvements to exclude the construction of advertisements.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Allowing More Transparency in 
Reviewing Advertising Construction Act'' or the ``AMTRAK Act''.

SEC. 2. LIMITATION ON EXEMPTION FOR AMTRAK FROM CERTAIN LEGAL 
              REQUIREMENTS.

    Section 24902(j) of title 49, United States Code, is amended by 
adding at the end the following:
    ``(k) Limitation on Exemptions.--Subsection (j) shall not apply to 
an improvement activity carried out for the purpose of construction or 
display of an advertisement.''.
