                                 A BILL


 
To extend the period during which vessels that are shorter than 79 feet 
  in length and fishing vessels are not required to have a permit for 
      discharges incidental to the normal operation of the vessel.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. EXTENSION OF MORATORIUM.

    Section 2(a) of Public Law 110-299 (33 U.S.C. 1342 note) is amended 
by striking ``January 19, 2018'' and inserting ``March 23, 2018''.
