                                 A BILL


 
 To declare nuclear energy to be clean energy, for purposes of Federal 
                                  law.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. DECLARATION.

    For purposes of Federal law, nuclear energy is hereby declared to 
be clean energy, and any provision of Federal law relating to clean 
energy shall be considered to include nuclear energy.
