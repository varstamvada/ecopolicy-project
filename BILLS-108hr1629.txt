                                 A BILL


 
To clarify that the Upper Missouri River Breaks National Monument does 
not include within its boundaries any privately owned property, and for 
                            other purposes.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. PRIVATELY OWNED PROPERTY EXCLUDED FROM MONUMENT.

    (a) Short Title.--This section may be cited as the `Upper Missouri 
River Breaks Boundary Clarification Act'.
    (b) Privately Owned Property Excluded From Monument.--
            (1) Property clarification.--The Upper Missouri River 
        Breaks National Monument, established by Presidential 
        proclamation 7398 on January 17, 2001, shall not include within 
        its boundaries any privately owned property.
            (2) Map.--The Secretary shall keep a map of the Monument 
        reflecting the boundary clarification in paragraph (1) on file 
        and available for public inspection in the appropriate offices 
        of the Director of the Bureau of Land Management, Department of 
        the Interior.
