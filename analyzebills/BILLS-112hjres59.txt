                            JOINT RESOLUTION


 
 Disapproving a rule submitted by the Environmental Protection Agency 
relating to ``National Emission Standards for Hazardous Air Pollutants 
 for Area Sources: Industrial, Commercial, and Institutional Boilers''.

    Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United 
States of America in Congress assembled, That Congress disapproves the 
rule submitted by the Environmental Protection Agency relating to 
``National Emission Standards for Hazardous Air Pollutants for Area 
Sources: Industrial, Commercial, and Institutional Boilers'' (published 
at 76 Federal Register 15554 (March 21, 2011)), and such rule shall 
have no force or effect.
