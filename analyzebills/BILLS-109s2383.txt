                                 A BILL


 
  To amend the Safe, Accountable, Flexible, Efficient Transportation 
     Equity Act: A Legacy for Users to make a technical correction.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. CONTRACT AUTHORITY.

    (a) In General.--Section 1940 of the Safe, Accountable, Flexible, 
Efficient Transportation Equity Act: A Legacy for Users (Public Law 
109-59; 119 Stat. 1511) is amended by adding at the end the following:
    ``(c) Contract Authority.--Funds made available to carry out this 
section shall be available for obligation in the same manner as if the 
funds were apportioned under chapter 1 of title 23, United States 
Code.''.
    (b) Recision.--Of the unobligated balances of funds apportioned to 
States under chapter 1 of title 23, United States Code, $50,000,000 is 
rescinded.
