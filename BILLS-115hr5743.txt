                                 A BILL


 
  To prohibit the National Science Foundation from conducting certain 
 studies on relationships between Members of Congress with respect to 
           the sharing of guidance and informative documents.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. PROHIBITION ON USE OF FUNDS FOR CERTAIN STUDIES BY THE 
              NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION.

    No Federal funds may be used by the National Science Foundation to 
conduct a study on--
            (1) how Members of Congress set priorities within the 
        constraints of the contemporary legislature;
            (2) the relationships between Members of Congress with 
        respect to the sharing of guidance and informative documents; 
        or
            (3) the impact of such guidance and informative documents, 
        including dear colleague letters, sent between Members of 
        Congress.
