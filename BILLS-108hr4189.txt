                                 A BILL


 
   To modify and improve the funding structure of the Environmental 
                      Quality Incentives Program.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Full Enrollment Environmental 
Quality Incentives Program (FEEQIP) Act of 2004''.

SEC. 2. FUNDING OF THE ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY INCENTIVES PROGRAM.

    Section 1241(a)(6) of the Food Security Act of 1985 (16 U.S.C. 
3821(a)(6)) is amended by striking all that follows ``chapter 4'' and 
inserting a period.
