                                 A BILL


 
         To reinstate Department of Energy Order No. 202-03-2.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. CROSS SOUND CABLE ORDER.

     Notwithstanding Department of Energy Order No. 202-03-4, issued by 
the Secretary of Energy on May 7, 2004, or any other provision of law, 
Department of Energy Order No. 202-03-2, issued by the Secretary of 
Energy on August 28, 2003, is reinstated effective on the date of 
enactment of this Act and shall remain in effect unless rescinded by 
Act of Congress.
