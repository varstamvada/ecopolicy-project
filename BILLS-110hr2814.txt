                                 A BILL


 
To authorize the Secretary of Energy to provide loan guarantees for 100 
   percent of the cost of construction of new domestic nuclear power 
                         production facilities.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. NUCLEAR PLANT LOAN GUARANTEES.

    Section 1702(c) of the Energy Policy Act of 2005 (42 U.S.C. 
16512(c)) is amended by adding at the end the following: ``The 
Secretary may guarantee a loan for 100 percent of the cost of 
construction of new domestic nuclear power production facilities under 
this title.''.
