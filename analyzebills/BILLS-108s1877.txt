                                 A BILL


 
  To amend the Food Security Act of 1985 to improve the enrollment of 
  cropland into the conservation reserve program through the farmable 
                           wetlands program.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. FARMABLE WETLANDS PROGRAM.

    (a) Inclusion of Playas.--Section 1231(h)(2)(A)(i) of the Food 
Security Act of 1985 (16 U.S.C. 3831(h)(2)(A)(i)) is amended by 
inserting after ``section 1222(b)(1)(A)'' the following: ``and a playa 
(regardless of whether the playa is listed on a map described in 
subparagraph (B))''.
    (b) Maximum Size of Wetland.--Section 1231(h)(2)(D)(i)(I) of the 
Food Security Act of 1985 (16 U.S.C. 3831(h)(2)(D)(i)(I)) is amended--
            (1) by striking ``10 contiguous acres'' and inserting ``40 
        contiguous acres''; and
            (2) by striking ``5 acres'' and inserting ``20 acres''.
