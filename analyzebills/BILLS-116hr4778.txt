   To amend the Department of the Interior, Environment, and Related 
 Agencies Appropriations Act, 2008, to extend the authority to collect 
          Shasta-Trinity Marina fees through fiscal year 2027.

United States of America in Congress assembled,

    Section 422 of the Department of the Interior, Environment, and 
Related Agencies Appropriations Act, 2008 (Public Law 110-161; 121 Stat 
2149) is amended by striking ``2019''' and inserting ``2027''.
