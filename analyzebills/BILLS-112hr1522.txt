                                 A BILL


 
      To repeal the Energy Independence and Security Act of 2007.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. REPEAL OF ENERGY INDEPENDENCE AND SECURITY ACT OF 2007.

    The Energy Independence and Security Act of 2007 (Public Law 110-
140) is repealed, and the laws amended by that Act shall be read as if 
the amendments made by that Act were not enacted.
