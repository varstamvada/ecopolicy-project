                                 A BILL


 
To require the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency to 
study State efforts to regulate certain uses of nitrous oxide, and for 
                            other purposes.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Myles Edward Scott Act''.

SEC. 2. REPORT.

    Not later than 1 year after the date of enactment of this Act, the 
Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency, in consultation 
with the Secretary of Transportation and the Commissioner of the Food 
and Drug Administration, shall submit to Congress a report containing--
            (1) the results of a study of State regulatory requirements 
        with respect to nitrous oxide that is manufactured and sold for 
        use in automotive applications; and
            (2) recommendations for Federal requirements with respect 
        to such manufacture and sale that are needed to protect human 
        health and safety.
