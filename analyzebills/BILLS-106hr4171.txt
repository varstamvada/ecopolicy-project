                                 A BILL


 
 To amend title 49, United States Code, relating to the transportation 
                        of hazardous materials.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled, That section 5103 of 
title 49, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the 
following:
    ``(c) Nonapplication to Food.--No designation, regulation, or 
requirement prescribed under this chapter shall be applicable to a food 
under section 201 of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (21 
U.S.C. 321) and as specifically described in section 5002(a)(8) of the 
Internal Revenue Code of 1986, section 117(a)(5) of the Federal Alcohol 
Administration Act (27 U.S.C. 211(a)(5)), and part 5 of title 27, Code 
of Federal Regulations.''.
