                                 A BILL


 
   To amend title 49, United States Code, relating to the assurance 
required of owners and operators of airports with respect to long-term 
                  leases for construction of hangars.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. HANGAR CONSTRUCTION.

    Section 47107(a)(21) of title 49, United States Code, is amended by
