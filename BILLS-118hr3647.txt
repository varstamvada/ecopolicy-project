                                 A BILL


 
 To amend the FAA Reauthorization Act of 2018 to reauthorize aviation 
        workforce development programs, and for other purposes.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Aviation Investment and Recruitment 
Act of 2023'' or the ``AIR Act of 2023''.

SEC. 2. AVIATION WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT PROGRAMS.

    Section 625(b)(1) of the FAA Reauthorization Act of 2018 (49 U.S.C. 
40101 note) is amended--
            (1) by striking ``$5,000,000'' each place it appears and 
        inserting ``$20,000,000''; and
            (2) by striking ``2019 through 2023'' each place it appears 
        and inserting ``2024 through 2028''.
