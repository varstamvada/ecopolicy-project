                                 A BILL


 
To amend title 49, United States Code, to allow States to regulate tow 
                           truck operations.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled, That section 
14501(c)(2)(C) is amended by striking ``the price of'' and all that 
follows through ``transportation is'' and inserting ``regulation of tow 
truck operations whether or not such operations are''.
