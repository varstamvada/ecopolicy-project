                                 A BILL


 
 To revise the boundaries of John H. Chafee Coastal Barrier Resources 
                      System Ponce Inlet Unit P08.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. REPLACEMENT OF CERTAIN JOHN H. CHAFEE COASTAL BARRIER 
              RESOURCES SYSTEM MAP.

    (a) In General.--The map subtitled ``P08'', relating to the John H. 
Chafee Coastal Barrier Resources System unit designated as Coastal 
Barrier Resources System Ponce Inlet Unit P08, that is included in the 
set of maps entitled ``John H. Chafee Coastal Barrier Resources 
System'' and referred to in section 4(a) of the Coastal Barrier 
Resources Act (16 U.S.C. 3503(a)), is hereby replaced by another map 
relating to the unit entitled ``John H. Chafee Coastal Barrier 
Resources System Ponce Inlet Unit P08'' and dated _____.
    (b) Availability.--The Secretary of the Interior shall keep the 
replacement map referred to in subsection (a) on file and available for 
inspection in accordance with the provisions of section 4(b) of the 
Coastal Barrier Resources Act (16 U.S.C. 3503(b)).
