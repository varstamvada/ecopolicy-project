                                 A BILL


 
 To direct the Secretary of the Interior to correct a map relating to 
the Coastal Barrier Resources System Unit P31, located near the city of 
                         Mexico Beach, Florida.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. CORRECTION OF MAP RELATING TO COASTAL BARRIER RESOURCES 
              SYSTEM UNIT P31.

    (a) In General.--Not later than 30 days after the date of enactment 
of this Act, the Secretary of the Interior shall correct the map 
described in subsection (b) so that the depiction on that map of the 
boundary for Unit P31 on the portion of Mexico Beach located 
immediately south of Highway 98 and east of the city of Mexico Beach, 
Florida, is the same as the depiction of that boundary on the map for 
that unit that was in effect on September 20, 1982.
    (b) Map Described.--The map referred to in subsection (a) is the 
map included in a set of maps entitled ``Coastal Barrier Resources 
System'', dated October 24, 1990, that relates to Unit P31 of the 
Coastal Barrier Resources System, and is entitled ``The St. Andrew 
Complex P31''.
